No Account Exists on Our Servers

Parzi is architected as a 100% private, client-side application without user accounts. We do not store names, emails, passwords, conversation histories, or IP addresses in any central server database. Because there are no server-side accounts, there is no account to delete from our servers.

Where Is Your Data Stored?

All chats, prompts, model keys, and configuration parameters are stored exclusively in your own browser's localStorage on this computer or phone.

Manual Deletion Instructions

You can also clear your data manually at any time:

  • Inside Parzi: Go to Settings → Privacy & About → Click 🗑️ Wipe All Data.
  • In Chrome / Edge: Open DevTools (F12) → Application → Local Storage → Right-click and choose Clear.
  • In Firefox: Open DevTools (F12) → Storage → Local Storage → Delete all items.
  • In Mobile Browsers: Clear site data or history for this domain in your browser settings.
