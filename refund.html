Last updated: November 2026

1. Free & Open Access

Parzi is completely free to use. We do not charge subscription fees, recurring memberships, or one-time license fees for accessing the web client or downloading the companion server code.

2. Payment Processing

We do not collect credit cards, banking details, or billing information. No financial transactions are processed on or through Parzi.

3. Third-Party Provider Charges

If you supply custom API keys for third-party inference services (such as Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter) or paid map tile tokens (such as Cesium Ion, TomTom Developer), billing is handled directly through those respective third-party providers. Any refund requests or billing inquiries regarding API consumption must be addressed directly with those external vendors.

4. Inquiries

Because no fees are collected by Parzi, there are no direct refunds to issue. For questions or technical assistance regarding billing with connected providers, please reach out below.
